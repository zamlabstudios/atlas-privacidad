Privacy Policy

ATLAS: Frentes de Guerra · ZamLab Studios · Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Versión en español

In short: the game does not ask you for an account, does not collect personal data, has no ads or analytics, and stores your progress only on your own device. If you buy coins or packs (optional), the payment is processed by Huawei.

1. Who we are

ZamLab Studios ("we") develops the turn-based strategy game ATLAS: Frentes de Guerra ("the game"). For any question about this policy, write to us at zamlab.studios@gmail.com.

2. What information is stored and where

So that you can continue where you left off, the game stores in your device's local storage:

  • your game in progress (territories, troops, turn, difficulty and game news);
  • your game profile (coins, upgrades, arsenal and chosen difficulty);
  • the commander name you type and the rank you choose;
  • your settings (for example, whether you want to see the summary of each turn, and your language);
  • if you buy coins or packs, the identifiers of purchases already delivered (so they are not delivered twice), your coin balance and what your packs contain (bombs, tanks, planes and ships).

This information is not sent to us or to any third party. It stays on your device.

3. What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising identifiers or usage data. The game has no user accounts, shows no ads and includes no third-party analytics or tracking tools.

4. Permissions

The Android app requests only the internet and network state permissions, which the Huawei purchase service needs. The whole game is packaged inside the app and can be played offline; it does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

5. Purchases

In the AppGallery version you can optionally buy coin packs and military equipment packs (bombs, tanks, planes and ships) for the game. The purchase and payment are processed by Huawei (HUAWEI IAP) under its own terms and privacy policy: we do not receive your payment or contact data, only the signed confirmation that the purchase was completed, which we verify on your device before delivering the coins. What you buy (coins and packs) is stored on your device: if you erase the game data or reinstall it, it is lost. To avoid losing it you can generate a backup code in Settings and keep it yourself (for example, in your notes): it is text the game shows on screen and is not sent to any server or to us. For questions about charges or refunds, contact Huawei AppGallery support.

6. Third parties

The game does not share information with third parties, except what is necessary for purchases: Huawei (HMS Core / AppGallery services) processes payment data and your HUAWEI ID account data according to its own policy, which is outside our control. If you download it from an app store (for example, AppGallery), the store may process data about your download under its own policies. If you play a web version, the site hosting it may record technical visits (such as the IP address), like any website; the game adds no analytics.

7. Minors

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including minors. The game is a strategy game with a fictional war theme; check the age rating shown in the store before installing it.

8. Your control: erase your data

You can erase all of the game's saved data at any time from within the game: About & privacy → "Erase my saved data". It is also removed when you uninstall the app. Since we hold no copy of your information, there is nothing you need to request from us in order to delete it.

9. Security

Your game data is on your device and is protected by the device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

10. Changes to this policy

If we change it, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change affects how your information is handled, we will also tell you inside the game.

11. Contact
